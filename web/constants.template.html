The seven that define everything else

Since 2019 the SI has no artefacts and no measured base units. Seven constants are fixed by decree — assigned exact values with zero uncertainty — and every unit follows from them. The kilogram is no longer a cylinder in Paris; it is whatever makes Planck's constant come out right.
