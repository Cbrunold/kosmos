Timeline of the Universe

From the Planck epoch to the heat death, ordered on the only axis that can hold it: the logarithm of seconds since the beginning. The first second gets nine entries and the following hundred trillion years get one, because that is genuinely where the action is.

From the Cosmic Timeline database in Notion. Times and temperatures follow the standard ΛCDM chronology on Planck 2018 parameters. Everything past the present day is a projection, not a measurement — the far-future entries are order-of-magnitude estimates, and at least one of them (proton decay) may never happen at all.
