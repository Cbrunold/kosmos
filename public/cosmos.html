The Local Group

Everything gravitationally bound to us: two big spirals, a third smaller one, and forty-odd dwarfs strung out to the edge of the group's reach. Radius on the map is true distance on a log scale — each ring is ten times further out than it looks — and the angle groups objects by what they orbit, so it is not a direction on the sky.

Researchers on file

From the Cosmology databases in Notion. Observatory founding years and discovery years are stored as numbers rather than dates: ground sites are known to the year and space telescopes to the day, and one date column would have to either invent the former or discard the latter. Missions are the exception and keep real dates, because a launch is an instant somebody recorded — Apollo 11 left at 13:32 UTC on 16 July 1969. A mission with no end date is still running.
